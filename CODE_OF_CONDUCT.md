# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, professional, and privacy-conscious collaboration space. Participants are expected to communicate constructively, respect engagement boundaries, and avoid sharing sensitive information.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when giving feedback.
- Protect client, colleague, and contributor privacy.
- Challenge ideas without attacking people.
- Follow applicable laws, engagement authorization, and repository safety boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, sharing confidential or personal data, or encouraging unauthorized or harmful security activity is not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository owner using the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled discreetly. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect people, privacy, and the repository's defensive purpose.
